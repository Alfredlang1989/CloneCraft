# M09 contract - Macro biomes, oceans and inland lakes

## Purpose

Port the useful preserved worldgen behavior onto the current
`MODS/Default` field/pass architecture: planetary-scale ocean distribution,
real inland lake basins, larger biome geography, and the savanna/massif
rebalance. This is a port, not a cherry-pick of the former `data/` layout or an
excuse to replace the accepted worldgen contracts.

M09 is its own end-of-spine milestone. It does not include hierarchical fast
travel; that is M10.

## Required architecture

- obey `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and the current `docs/WORLDGEN.md` pipeline;
- persistent/worldgen coordinates remain exact hierarchy digits and are never
  flattened to one global integer or absolute floating-point position;
- seeds fold all hierarchy digits deterministically;
- biome, hydrology, material and scale choices live in content data/Lua under
  `MODS/Default`, not as biome/content-name switches in generic C++;
- current field validation, pass stages, priorities, replacement tags and
  bounded chunk-generation contracts remain authoritative;
- the result is independent of chunk request order, worker scheduling and
  neighbouring chunk residency;
- do not blindly cherry-pick preserved commits: their `data/` paths, loader
  shape and ABI predate the current content root and tests.

## Required geography

### Macro oceans

Ocean/land selection combines a very-low-frequency continental signal with
smaller coast roughness. It must form coherent ocean bodies and continents,
not per-biome blue patches. Ocean floor material, water level and minimum
water-bottom behavior are expressed through normal fields and passes.

### Inland lakes

A lake is physical generated geometry, not a biome colour or mask alone. The
content graph provides at least:

- inland lake candidate/strength;
- lake level;
- lake bottom;
- water bottom;
- shore mask.

The pass order is explicit and deterministic: carve basin, place bed, shape
shore, then fill water. Lake eligibility must keep candidates inland and may
use rain/geology/lowland signals so lakes do not erase coastlines or drown the
world.

### Larger biomes

Biome masks operate at world-scale spacing suitable for exploration. Savanna
and massif geography from the preserved branch is recovered where it still
fits the current registry/terrain model. Boundaries may have detail, but the
dominant biome footprint must not collapse back into small noisy patches.

## Porting packet

The preserved implementation already establishes the intended algorithmic
shape. Port this behavior into current files instead of re-inventing it:

```text
land_signal  = macro_continent * 0.78 + coast_region * 0.17 + coast_detail * 0.05
land_factor  = smoothstep(land_low, land_high, land_signal)
ocean_mask   = 1 - land_factor

lake_strength = max(huge, large, medium candidates)
                * inland_gate * wet_gate * lowland_gate
lake_bottom   = lake_level - configured_depth

passes = lake_carve -> lake_bed -> lake_shore -> lake_water -> ocean_water
```

Thresholds, scales and salts are starting reference values, not hidden C++
constants. Tune them in current content with deterministic morphology tests.

## Mandatory proofs

1. fixed seed/address samples are bit-for-bit deterministic across repeated
   runs and chunk request order;
2. negative coordinates and hierarchy seams do not change field continuity or
   seed identity;
3. generated output contains coherent land and macro ocean coverage without
   collapsing to all-land or all-water;
4. lake candidates produce carved basins containing actual water blocks, a
   bottom and a shore transition;
5. lake water does not float above uncarved terrain or leak merely because a
   neighbouring chunk was generated later;
6. ocean and lake material passes obey current stage/priority/replacement-tag
   rules and do not overwrite protected content;
7. quantitative morphology tests prove larger dominant biome regions and
   useful savanna/massif coverage without relying only on a screenshot;
8. chunk-boundary samples and separately generated neighbouring chunks agree;
9. the current content root is `MODS/Default`; no active dependency on the old
   `data/` layout is introduced;
10. no biome id, water content id or geography mode is special-cased in generic
    C++;
11. existing worldgen/registry/streaming regressions remain green;
12. the independent reviewer gate `./compile.sh` passes on the target
    environment, followed by a human map/world inspection.
